# Publication venues for the AdiletCodex data descriptor

Goal: publish a **data descriptor** for AdiletCodex in a journal that is **free to
publish in for KBTU authors** (APC covered by KBTU's IEEE open-access agreement), rather
than paying an APC at MDPI Data / Data in Brief.

Source: KBTU-IEEE open-access journal lists (IEEE Title List + IEEE Full-Open-Access list),
provided by the author 2026-08-21.

## Recommended (top choice)

**IEEE Data Descriptions** - Full Open Access.
- IEEE's dedicated journal for dataset descriptors (the IEEE counterpart of *Scientific Data*
  / *Data in Brief*). AdiletCodex fits its scope directly, as a descriptor - no need to
  reframe the work as a research/methods paper.
- New journal, so no Journal Impact Factor assigned yet, but it is the official IEEE data
  journal and is indexed.
- Full Open Access -> covered (free) under the KBTU-IEEE agreement.
- **Action:** confirm with the KBTU library that the agreement waives the APC for Full-OA
  IEEE titles (not only hybrid). Then submit under their data-descriptor template.

## Alternatives (also on the KBTU-IEEE lists, free)

- **IEEE Open Journal of the Computer Society (OJCS)** - Full OA, JIF 6.0. Accepts
  resource/dataset papers. A draft already exists (`../2.uz-high-court/paper_ojcs_b`).
  Good if a journal with an already-assigned Impact Factor is preferred.
- **IEEE Transactions on Big Data** - Hybrid OA, JIF 5.7. Higher impact, but needs a genuine
  research contribution beyond the dataset (covered under read-and-publish for hybrid).
- **IEEE Transactions on Computational Social Systems** - Hybrid OA, JIF 4.9. Same caveat.
- (For reference) **IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering** - Hybrid, JIF 10.4;
  research paper, not a descriptor.

## Notes
- "Free" depends on the exact KBTU-IEEE agreement type: read-and-publish typically waives the
  APC for **hybrid** journals; **Full-OA** journals (Data Descriptions, OJCS, Access) may be
  under a separate arrangement - verify with the KBTU library before committing.
- After the descriptor is published, add its DOI to the Zenodo record's **Related
  identifiers** (dataset concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22812625, version 10.5281/zenodo.22812626).

Decision: pending author confirmation (leaning IEEE Data Descriptions).
